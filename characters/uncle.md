# The Uncle  `PROPOSED` (name is a placeholder)

**Working name:** Uncle Ray (Raymond Calloway), late 60s. Relationship to
narrator: his father's older brother (or mother's, TBD).

## Snapshot
Retired founder. Built and sold a company (placeholder: industrial/enterprise
software or a hardware firm, sold in the early 2000s or 2010s), then walked away
quietly. Lives in a modest house in the Santa Cruz mountains, away from the tech spotlight, on a larger-than-most backyard he is forever landscaping (new flowerbeds, water features), with one small vegetable garden that was Denise's.

## Backstory (draft)
- Founded his company at 31 with a college friend. Nearly went under twice.
- **Marriage:** Married to Denise for 38 years until she died 4 years ago. (Decided: widowed.) His
  marriage taught him more than the company did, and he knows it.
- **Regret:** His business partner (the college friend) drifted away after a
  fight neither of them repaired. Ray is careful about what he says on that
  subject.
- **What he learned:** Companies fail in the silence between people more than
  in the market.

## Function in the story
- Sounding board, not oracle. His advice is well intentioned; Nate decides whether to follow it.
- Comic and emotional engine: the narrator arrives with a "business problem."
  Ray asks a question that reframes it. Never says out loud that he knows.
  Sometimes a small gesture gives it away (a raised eyebrow, offering tea,
  pausing before answering).
- Aging: his health or a decision about his house gives late-book stakes.

## Voice
Unhurried. Short sentences. Analogies from landscaping (drainage, slope, retaining walls), machinery, old customers.
Asks more than tells. Never uses startup jargon unless mocking it.

## The recurring pattern
1. Narrator: "Hypothetically, if a cofounder..."
2. Ray: pours coffee, waits.
3. Ray answers the business question completely and precisely, in a way that
   also answers the real one.
4. Narrator drives home realizing it.

## Arc
Starts as a mentor who gives advice; ends as a man who receives some
(the couple caring for him, or the couple being the ones to say a hard thing to
him).
