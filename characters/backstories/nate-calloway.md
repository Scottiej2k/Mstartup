# Nate Calloway: Full Backstory  `PROPOSED`

Age 28 at open. Founder/CEO of Loopback.health. Narrator.

## The family he came from
- **Dayton, Ohio.** Only child (proposed; see questions). Modest brick ranch on a
  street where everyone mowed on Saturday.
- **Dan Calloway (father), now ~60.** Facilities manager for a regional hospital
  system. He fixes things before anyone knows they're broken. Never late, never
  says "I love you." Says "Text me when you get there."
- **Carol Calloway (mother), now ~58.** Dental office scheduler for 30 years. She
  runs the calendar of the entire extended family. Her love language is a
  color-coded wall calendar.
- Home rule: *feelings are handled privately, problems are handled together.* Nate
  learned that a problem with a solution is a form of affection.

## Formative moments
1. **Age 8, the list.** Dan's father (Grandpa Walt Calloway, since deceased) had a
   stroke. The family crisis was handled with a legal pad: who drives, who
   cooks, who calls. Nate watched the list calm everyone down. He has made a list
   ever since. Lists are how he feels safe.
2. **Age 12, the broken dishwasher.** He took it apart with his dad on a Saturday.
   Best day of his childhood: side by side, hands busy, nobody talking. He
   still equates closeness with working on something together.
3. **Age 15, the science fair.** He built a pill-organizer prototype for
   his grandmother, a cheap reminder box. It won regionally. (Quiet seed for the
   healthcare startup, which Nate has half forgotten.)
4. **Age 17, Uncle Ray's visit.** Ray flew in for a funeral and spent an evening
   asking Nate what he wanted to build. Nobody had asked him a question like
   that. Ray told him, "Wanting to build something is not the same as wanting
   to leave. But you might need to leave to find out."
5. **Age 22, the breakup line.** College girlfriend Hannah, after two years: "You're
   a great planner. I never felt like I was in your plan." He took it as a
   scheduling problem and fixed his calendar.

## Education and work
- Full-ride engineering scholarship (Ohio state university, unnamed in the book).
- First job: enterprise software at a large company in Columbus. Left after 2.5 years,
  bored by the slowness and by never seeing a user.
- Moved to the Bay at 24. Roommates: Kyle (an old college friend) and one
  transient engineer. Cofounded with Priya, whom he met at his first job.
- A pivot: the first idea (an inventory tool) died in 8 months. Loopback is idea #2.
  He learned the hard way that he'd built the first one without talking to a single
  customer.

## Uncle Ray
- Dan's older brother by seven years. Ray left Ohio at 22; Dan stayed to look after
  their parents. Dan is proud of Ray, resents him a little, and says neither.
- After Nate moved out west, Ray became his Sunday-dinner anchor. Nate calls it
  "mentorship." Ray calls it "coffee."

## Psychological profile
- **Core belief:** love is earned through output.
- **Coping:** plans, lists, optimization, humor as a shield.
- **Blind spot:** treats people's needs as tickets to close. He thinks he is being
  attentive when he is being efficient.
- **Tell:** when nervous he explains. When hurt he goes quiet and gets *productive.*
- **What he's proud of:** being reliable.
- **What he's afraid of:** that reliability is all there is.

## The Valley version of Nate
- Mountain View apartment with Kyle: two roommates' worth of mess, one dying plant.
- Runs on cold brew and a Notion page called "Life OS," which Maya later discovers
  contains a section titled "Relationships" (unfilled).
- Doesn't drink much at events, holds a cup of water so he has something to do
  with his hands.

## Arc beats (backstory tie-ins)
- **Ch 1:** He meets Maya holding a water cup. She's the first person who asks about
  his product without asking about his valuation.
- **Ch 4:** Ray's first "hypothetically": he's actually asking about Maya.
- **Ch 8:** First deliberate choice to commit; he makes a spreadsheet and then throws
  it away.
- **Ch 19:** Maya's line ("Am I a project?") hits Hannah's line from six years ago.
- **Ch 22:** Ray's questions, and Nate finally hears "I want to marry her" out loud.
