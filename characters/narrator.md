# Narrator  `PROPOSED` (all names are placeholders)

**Working name:** Nate Calloway, 28 at open. (Placeholder; Scott to choose.)

## Snapshot
Founder and CEO of an early-stage startup. Smart, driven, funny in a defensive
way, better at systems than at people. Believes problems are solved by working
harder and being more rational.

## Backstory (draft)
- **Origin:** Grew up in a mid-sized Midwestern town (placeholder: Dayton, Ohio)
  in a stable, low-drama, emotionally quiet household. Love was shown through
  logistics: packed lunches, oil changes, never being late. Nobody said
  "I love you"; they said "text me when you get there."
- **School:** Full ride to a strong engineering program. First in his family to
  work in tech. Took a job at a large company after graduating, hated the
  slowness, left after 2.5 years to found something.
- **Arrived in the Valley:** at 24, sharing a cramped apartment with two
  roommates in Mountain View, sleeping four hours a night, and calling it a
  personality.
- **Past relationships:** One serious college girlfriend who told him, kindly,
  "You're a great planner. I never felt like I was in your plan." He never
  really processed it. Since then he dated in the gaps between sprints.
- **The wound:** He believes love is earned through output. He doesn't know he
  believes this.

## Want vs. need
- **Want:** to build something big enough to prove that leaving home and
  everything safe was worth it.
- **Need:** to learn that presence isn't a resource you allocate; it's the
  point.

## Flaws
Defaults to fixing instead of listening; treats vulnerability like a risk to
manage; goes quiet under stress; keeps score when he thinks he's being generous.

## Strengths
Loyal, honest about facts, genuinely curious once he slows down, willing to
change his mind if shown clear evidence (this is how Maya wins him).

## Voice
