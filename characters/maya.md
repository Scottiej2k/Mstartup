# Maya  `PROPOSED` (surname and details are placeholders)

**Working name:** Maya Raman, 27 at open.

## Snapshot
Sharp, warm in a way that surprises people, allergic to performative networking.
Came to the event under protest: a friend or colleague dragged her, or her
employer sent her. She is the least impressed person in the room, and it
isn't an act.

## Backstory (draft)
- **Family:** Raised in the Bay Area (Fremont) by parents who immigrated and
  built a small business (placeholder: a family-run pharmacy). She grew up doing
  the books at the counter. She understands small business more deeply than
  anyone at the event, and hates being condescended to about "hustle."
- **Education/Career:** Studied something adjacent to tech but not tech
  (placeholder: public health or design research), now works somewhere
  meaningful and unglamorous (placeholder: UX researcher at a healthcare
  nonprofit, or a hospital-systems analyst). She is not at the event to network;
  she is there because someone she owes a favor asked.
- **Why she dislikes these events:** She's watched people treat conversation like
  extraction. Her father's customers were people, not "leads."
- **Past relationships:** Dated a founder once. Ended after he treated her time
  as infinite and her opinions as decorative. She swore off the genre.
- **The wound:** She's been the dependable one her whole life; she is quietly
  afraid of needing anyone and not being met.

## Want vs. need
- **Want:** a life she chooses on purpose, not one she defaults into.
- **Need:** to let someone show up for her without earning it first.

## Flaws
Withdraws instead of fighting when hurt; tests people silently; sometimes
mistakes self-sufficiency for strength.

## Strengths
Curious, perceptive, funny, principled, an excellent question-asker. Her
instincts about people are almost always right, and that's a plot
problem for a narrator who likes evidence.

## Meet-cute (APPROVED)
Her mother told her to get out more, and sent her to the founder/investor mixer
to scout new technology that might help the family pharmacy. She agreed
begrudgingly.

## Her own arc (APPROVED)
Her parents decide to expand the pharmacy, which doubles her work. They refuse
to hire anyone to help her, which creates tension with them, and with Nate when he
tries to solve it. She is not the "support system" for his startup; at times she
is the one making the harder sacrifice, and the book must acknowledge that.

## The turning point (APPROVED)
In Part 5 she tells Nate she's tired of being treated like his startup: a
project, a grind, boxes to check. He's a good partner with flaws, and hadn't
realized it until she said it.

## Dynamic
He plans. She notices. He talks. She asks. He solves. She waits to see if he
will stay in the discomfort.
