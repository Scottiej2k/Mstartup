# Setting & Backdrop  `PROPOSED`

## Era
Contemporary, unspecific-but-recent (mid-2020s). Remote/hybrid norms, AI
everywhere in pitches, funding environment picky after the boom. (Option: set
in a slightly earlier era to avoid dating the book. See open questions.)

## Place
San Francisco Bay Area: Peninsula and South Bay for the company, the city for
nights out, the East Bay for Maya's family.

## Texture of the Valley (use with restraint)
- Every conversation opens with "So what are you working on?"
- Coffee chat as a full-time job
- Pitch decks, demo days, the language of "traction"
- Money appearing and disappearing on a Tuesday
- Housing costs as a silent third character in every relationship decision

## Social backdrop
Two worlds: the founder world (performing certainty) and Maya's world
(actually serving people). The book lets each judge the other, then learn from
it.

## Timeline of the book
~5 years from meeting to pregnancy reveal.

| Year | Company | Relationship |
|---|---|---|
| Y1 | Idea, prototype, first users | Meeting, dating, first real fight |
| Y2 | First customers, seed round | Exclusive, moving toward living together |
| Y3 | Growth and first crisis | Living together, near-breakup |
| Y4 | Series A / maturity | Engagement, wedding planning |
| Y5 | Established company, new direction | Marriage; pregnancy |
