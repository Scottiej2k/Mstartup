# Themes  `PROPOSED`

## Central idea
Building something that lasts, whether a company or a marriage, is mostly about
the unglamorous disciplines: honesty, listening, showing up, and repairing after
you get it wrong.

## Core themes
1. **Listening is customer discovery.** You can't build for someone you never
   asked. (Dating = interviewing without a script; marriage = never stopping.)
2. **Vision vs. ego.** Conviction gets companies started; refusing to update on
   evidence kills them. Same for relationships.
3. **Ambition and presence.** The founder's core tension: the work always feels
   urgent, the person always feels patient. Until she isn't.
4. **Transparency under pressure.** Hiding a bad quarter and hiding a bad day are
   the same mistake.
5. **Co-founding.** Partnership is not 50/50 every day; it's each carrying 100%
   when the other can't.
6. **Pivoting without abandoning.** You can change the plan and keep the
   commitment.
7. **Growth changes the thing you built.** Success forces you to renegotiate
   roles, time, and identity.
