To change a line or ask a question, select it.
  1. Select any words. A bar appears at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Comment opens a box where you tell Claude what to change, or ask a question, about the selected words. It joins the Queue tab as Submitted, and Claude is notified. You will see it move to Working, then Completed, with a note from Claude on what was done. Press Resolve when you are happy with it.
  3. Edit text (top right) lets you click into any paragraph and type your change directly. Press Enter to save that paragraph, or Esc to cancel. Each change joins the Queue, and Send to Claude asks Claude to apply your wording exactly as you wrote it.
  4. Highlight marks a passage for yourself, in this browser only. Copy puts the passage and its chapter on your clipboard.
